# Realistic TNT (Horizon native mod)

Explosions that look, sound and hit like the real thing: a fireball and a mushroom cloud, a visible
shockwave that hurts, throws and flattens what it sweeps over, a real crater, flying debris and a
delayed boom. TNT adds up with no limit.

The fireball, the mushroom cloud and the bent air of the shockwave are drawn by the companion shader
pack "Realistic TNT: explosions" (optional). The full documentation and a video are in the
repository: https://github.com/stealer0002/horizon-realistic-tnt
